# AI Extension Process

> The stage of building AI-specific license, transparency, and SBOM processes that extend beyond code to cover models, weights, and datasets.

---

LLMS index: [llms.txt](/llms.txt)

---

This is stage 2 of the implementation roadmap. It is the area where the AI SBOM guide extends most significantly
beyond ISO/IEC 5230, covering licensing obligations (3.5), transparency obligations (3.6), and AI SBOM
generation and management (3.9).

---

Section pages:

- [3.5 License Obligations](/en/docs/ai-sbom_guide/2-ai-extension/1-license-obligations/): Explains the procedure for reviewing the licenses of an AI system's code, weights, datasets, and model tree to determine obligations, restrictions, and rights.
- [3.6 Transparency Obligations](/en/docs/ai-sbom_guide/2-ai-extension/2-transparency-obligations/): Explains the procedure for reviewing transparency obligations imposed by regulation and applying risk mitigation measures to issues such as disclosure of training data.
- [3.9 AI SBOM](/en/docs/ai-sbom_guide/2-ai-extension/3-ai-sbom/): Explains the procedure and formats for generating and managing an AI SBOM, automation tools, and the verification areas that tools alone struggle to fill.
